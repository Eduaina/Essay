**FashionLink** was my capstone project, built as part of **Group 68 — Team S.T.E.M.inists**. It was my first time taking a React frontend multi-entry product from idea to a fully deployed product as part of a team. Actually I was the head of the technical team.


{/* truncate */}


## The project


FashionLink set out to connect people around fashion in a simple, accessible web app. As a team, we split up the frontend work, aligned on design decisions, and shipped it to production together.


## My role


I focused on the **React frontend**, working through:


- Component structure and reusable UI pieces
- Styling with CSS to match the team's design direction
- Coordinating with teammates via GitHub — commits, PRs, and merges as a group


## Tech stack


- **React** for the frontend (JavaScript)
- **CSS** for styling
- **Vercel** for deployment


## What I learned


Capstone projects hit different from solo ones — most of what I took away was about collaboration:


- Working in a shared codebase without stepping on teammates' changes
- Communicating scope and blockers early
- Balancing individual ownership of features with a consistent overall product
